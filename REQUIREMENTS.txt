================================================================================
Cleanix - Full-Stack SaaS Platform
Software Requirement Specification (SRS) & Production Roadmap
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTVIE SUMMARY & CORE CONCEPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cleanix is an on-demand B2B (Business-to-Business) and B2C (Business-to-Consumer)
hybrid cleaning service automation platform. It delivers reliable, fast, and 
high-quality cleaning services for residential homes, commercial offices, 
renovation sites, and relocation properties.

The platform is not just a simple booking site; it is a full SaaS (Software-as-a-Service)
driven field service management ecosystem where customers, service providers 
(cleaner teams), and administrators interact seamlessly.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TARGET AUDIENCE & REVENUE MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target Audience:
1. Residential Customers (B2C): Busy professionals and families needing regular or deep cleaning.
2. Commercial Customers (B2B): Corporate offices, showrooms, restaurants needing daily/weekly routine cleaning.
3. Relocating Customers (Move-in / Move-out): Property owners moving into or leaving a space.
4. Construction & Renovation Owners (Post-Construction): Property owners needing heavy dust & debris cleanup.

Revenue Generation Streams:
1. Recurring Subscription Packages:
   - Basic Plan ($199/month): Standard home cleaning, kitchen & bath refresh, email support.
   - Standard Plan ($499/month): Priority deep cleaning, sanitizing, 24/7 support, multi-room pack.
   - Premium Plan ($899/month): Master commercial & home clean, dedicated concierge, real-time tracking.
2. Custom Instant Estimate (One-Time Booking): Dynamic pay-per-service billing calculated based on square footage, room count, and selected add-ons.
3. Add-on Service Fees: Oven wash, fridge deep clean, interior window cleaning, pet hygiene treatment.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Frontend Framework: Next.js 16 (App Router)
- Styling & UI: Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion + Lenis Smooth Scroll
- Database: PostgreSQL (Supabase / Neon)
- ORM Layer: Prisma ORM
- Authentication: NextAuth.js (Auth.js) / Clerk (OAuth, Magic Link, RBAC Credentials)
- Payment Engine: Stripe API (Subscriptions, Payment Intents, Webhooks)
- Media & Storage: Uploadthing / Cloudinary (Before/After Photo Uploads)
- Communication API: Resend (Email) + Twilio (SMS Notifications)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Customer Role:
   - Select service category, space size, time slot, and book instantly.
   - Pay online via credit card or activate monthly subscription plans.
   - Live track job status (Scheduled, Assigned, En Route, Completed).
   - Download PDF invoices, leave ratings & reviews.

2. Cleaner / Staff Role:
   - View assigned daily jobs, schedules, and Google Maps directions.
   - Update job status (Check-in, In Progress, Completed).
   - Upload Before & After photos for quality assurance.

3. Admin / Dispatcher Role:
   - Visual Schedule Calendar: Assign or re-assign jobs to staff.
   - Dynamic Pricing Engine: Modify service fees, subscription prices, promo codes.
   - CRM & User Management: Manage customers, cleaners, and subscription statuses.
   - Financial Analytics Dashboard: Daily revenue, total bookings, staff payouts.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CORE FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Instant Booking & Dynamic Price Calculator:
   - Real-time algorithm calculates total bill based on rooms/bathrooms & sq. ft.
   - Time slot picker with real-time slot availability blocking.

2. Subscription & Auto-Billing Engine (Stripe Integration):
   - Automated monthly subscription billing with dunning/payment recovery emails.

3. Smart Cleaner Allocation & Dispatch:
   - Auto-notifies nearest available cleaner team based on customer location.

4. Proof of Work & Quality Control:
   - Mandatory photo upload upon job completion with instant customer rating prompt.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATABASE SCHEMA MODELS (PRISMA ORM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- User Model: id, name, email, password, role (CUSTOMER, CLEANER, ADMIN), phone, address
- Service Model: id, title, slug, basePrice, description, category
- Booking Model: id, bookingNumber, customerId, cleanerId, serviceId, status, totalAmount, scheduledAt, address, beforePhotos, afterPhotos, paymentStatus
- Subscription Model: id, userId, stripeSubId, planName, price, status, startDate, endDate
- Review Model: id, userId, rating, comment, createdAt


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 1: Database & Auth Setup (Week 1)
- Prisma ORM & PostgreSQL setup. Role-based auth (NextAuth.js).

Phase 2: Booking Engine & Pricing Logic (Week 2)
- Dynamic price calculator widget, time slot picker, Stripe integration.

Phase 3: Customer & Staff Portals (Week 3)
- Customer booking dashboard, field staff mobile dashboard with photo upload.

Phase 4: Admin Control Center & Deployment (Week 4)
- Visual schedule calendar, dispatch system, financial analytics, QA testing, production deployment.
